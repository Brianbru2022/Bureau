# Storefront Copy — Release Candidate

## Name

**The Bureau of Questionable Knowledge**

## Short description

A British shared-screen party quiz where knowledge, judgement and reckless administrative confidence all earn their day at the desk.

## Long description

Report for assessment at the least dependable government department never founded. One to four candidates compete across a changing selection of quiz departments: locate places, open archival shutters, estimate improbable figures, build risky lists, rank evidence and decide exactly when confidence has gone far enough.

The Bureau is entirely fictional and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government, public authority or the Royal Household.

Every assessment mixes familiar knowledge with judgement and risk. Questions are curated rather than generated live, scoring responds continuously to actual performance, and optional Office Politics adds private directives and bureaucratic interference without requiring phones or accounts.

Play solo for a personal appraisal or gather up to four candidates around one Windows PC. Choose a quick session, a standard assessment, the full departmental ordeal or a custom selection. The game works offline after installation and saves an interrupted assessment locally.

## Feature points

- One to four players on one shared Windows screen
- Seventeen quiz departments selected into replayable assessments
- Quick, Standard, Full and Custom formats
- Optional timers, Office Politics and host adjudication
- Keyboard, pointer and touch-compatible controls
- Local save and resume; no account and no gameplay telemetry
- Reduced-motion, scalable text and persistent sound controls

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
- 1366×768 minimum recommended desktop resolution
- Keyboard and mouse or touchpad; touch is supported on compatible displays
- No internet connection required for normal play

## Privacy line

No account, advertising or automatic analytics. Assessments and preferences remain on the local PC unless the player deliberately exports a recovery or consented beta-support file.
