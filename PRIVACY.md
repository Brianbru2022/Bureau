# Privacy notice

Last updated: 30 August 2026

The Bureau of Questionable Knowledge is designed as an offline, local shared-screen game. It does not require an account and does not send candidate names, scores, answers, play-test events or saved assessments to the developer.

## Data stored locally

The game may store settings, candidate names, active-match recovery data, recent challenge history, score history and adjudication history on the device. The Windows edition also keeps an atomic recovery copy and a local crash log in its per-user application-data folder.

Structured closed-beta diagnostics are stored only after the host confirms every participant's explicit consent. They consist of pseudonymous session configuration, timings, ratings and observer-marked incident categories. The beta export excludes candidate names, recovery data and free-text notes. It is created manually and is never transmitted automatically.

Players can clear saved game data and local beta evidence from the application. A host may explicitly export a recovery, JSON or CSV file; exported files remain under the host’s control.

## Network use

Normal play uses bundled fonts, photographs and map imagery and does not require an internet connection. The application contains no advertising, analytics or telemetry SDK. Storefronts, operating systems and download hosts may process their own technical or purchase data under their separate policies.

## Children and shared devices

The game does not deliberately collect personal data from children. Hosts should avoid entering sensitive information as candidate names, especially on shared computers or in exported files.

## Contact

The legal publisher and privacy contact must be inserted in `SUPPORT.md` before commercial distribution.
