## Intended support information

Support requests should include the product version, Windows version, whether the portable or installed edition is being used, and a description of the last completed action. The Windows title screen can export a privacy-safe support bundle containing technical versions, update state and a bounded crash-log tail. It excludes recovery records and candidate names. Players should not send recovery files unless specifically requested because they may contain candidate names and match history.

The release version must define its supported Windows versions, update period, refund route, response target and procedure for reporting accessibility, factual or licensing concerns.

The proposed operational response targets and patch cadence are recorded in `FIRST-MONTH-SUPPORT-PLAN.md`. They do not become public commitments until the legal publisher and support route above are completed and approved.
