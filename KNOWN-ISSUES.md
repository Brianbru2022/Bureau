# Known Issues — 1.0.0-rc.1

There are no known critical gameplay progression defects in the automated release-candidate journeys. That statement is limited to the automated evidence; independent closed-beta evidence is still outstanding.

Commercial distribution is currently blocked by the open records in `KNOWN-ISSUES.json`: legal publisher and support details, professional trade mark and policy review, generated-art clearance, independent editorial approval, Windows signing, a production update feed and representative clean-machine/full-match evidence. Official-looking Crown branding has been removed from the fictional product identity, but that product-side mitigation is not a legal opinion.

The closed-beta workflow and exact cohort verification are implemented. `PLAYTEST-CERTIFICATION.json` and `PLAYTEST-FINDINGS.md` remain at **AWAITING EVIDENCE** until real independent groups complete the three required sessions with full event trails. `BETA-BALANCE-REPORT.json` also awaits six-group coverage and eight eligible attempts per department; automated journeys are not substituted for those people.

An unsigned local candidate may show a Windows reputation warning. It must not be treated as the public installer. The application is designed for Windows 10 and Windows 11 on a shared local screen; online multiplayer, accounts and phone controllers are not supported.

New defects must be added to the JSON register with an owner, severity and resolution version before a go decision. Any open `CRITICAL`, `HIGH` or `RELEASE_BLOCKER` issue prevents release.
