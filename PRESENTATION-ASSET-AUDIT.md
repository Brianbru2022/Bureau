# Presentation asset audit

Stage 8 keeps final gameplay artwork in `public` and keeps source or superseded concepts outside the shipped site under `assets/source-art` and `assets/source-media`.

`pnpm presentation:audit` enforces the release-facing rules:

- every department and state resolves to an existing responsive WebP apparatus;
- every apparatus and Image Reveal photograph is below 400,000 bytes;
- reveal media is local WebP rather than a network dependency;
- the two reconnaissance licence registers match the shipped files;
- obsolete generated-v3 concepts and unreferenced generated-v2 concepts are not shipped;
- the internal artwork quality record covers all 17 departments.

Image Reveal photographs were converted from the retained source JPEGs to maximum 1440×1080 WebP derivatives. Their public weight fell from 10,679,194 bytes to 3,064,528 bytes (71.3%). Dynamic instructions, values and controls remain HTML/SVG, and failed decorative artwork is hidden without disabling the interaction.

The commercial Stage 8 pass retains the approved apparatus masters and reduces their decorative opacity so live information remains dominant. Every apparatus now names its active control in a text-backed brass legend. Processing, accepted, rejected and result states use a high-contrast textual ribbon as well as colour, lamps and mechanical overlays.

Department audio is original procedural synthesis rather than downloaded samples. Seventeen signatures are assembled from eight restrained physical motifs with filtered mechanical transients and compressed resonances. Mute, master and effects settings remain persistent, and no required state is conveyed by sound alone. `AUDIO-QUALITY-REVIEW.json` records the technical and rights review; independent listening sessions remain a human release gate.
